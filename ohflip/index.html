How to play

  • Click or tap anywhere to bounce.
  • Hold while you are in the air to spin - the longer you hold, the more flips you land.
  • Let go before you come down. Landing upright bounces you higher; landing crooked drops you off the trampoline and resets your height.
  • Each landing that completes the current goal moves you on to the next one.

The game has ? levels, each one a goal shown in the top right corner while you play.
